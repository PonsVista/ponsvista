# Screener guide

The Screener provides several views of Pons tokens. Each view answers a different question about the available launch and market data.

## Top

The [Top view](https://ponsvista.xyz/) orders tokens by circulating market cap. Market cap depends on the price and the circulating-supply observation available to the source. It is different from liquidity or trading volume.

## Volume

The [Volume view](https://ponsvista.xyz/volume) focuses on 24-hour trading volume. Compare entries using the same currency and time window. A pool-scoped value describes the indexed pool, rather than a guaranteed total across every venue.

## New

The [New view](https://ponsvista.xyz/new) lists recent launches, newest first. Launch age, curve progress and stage describe different parts of a token's launch record. The available rows depend on indexing and source coverage.

## Active

The [Active view](https://ponsvista.xyz/active) focuses on recent trading activity. Its order is not a market-cap ranking. Activity records can arrive later than the underlying events.

## Gainers

The [Gainers view](https://ponsvista.xyz/gainers) uses supported change since the first indexed trade. It is not simply the 24-hour change column. The quote currency and the available historical baseline are part of the result; incomplete history can limit which entries appear.

## Near graduation

The [Near graduation view](https://ponsvista.xyz/near-graduation) organizes curve-stage tokens using available curve progress. Progress is a reported launch-state observation. A displayed percentage is not a prediction about whether or when graduation will happen.

## Graduated

The [Graduated view](https://ponsvista.xyz/graduated) lists tokens identified as graduated in the available launch data. This label describes a launch stage, not an assessment of project quality or market performance.

## Search and filters

Search can help locate a token by its name, symbol or published contract address. Quote filters narrow the visible entries to supported pairs. Use the full contract address when distinguishing tokens with similar names.

## Token details

Token pages provide a closer look at the available price history, recent records and market information. Availability varies with the token, source and indexed history. Read timestamps, currencies and period labels alongside the values.

For metric definitions, see [Data notes](data-notes.md).
